How Fit Scores Work

Fit scores are computed by our deterministic weighted matching algorithm across 5 dimensions: Skill fit (45%), Domain interest (20%), Experience suitability (15%), Role alignment (10%), and Work preferences (10%). We never guarantee employment; scores represent estimated profile alignment.

Free-Tier & Offline Operation

If Gemini AI quota is busy or unavailable, CareerPath AI seamlessly falls back to Local Guidance Mode using our comprehensive local career catalog and algorithms.
